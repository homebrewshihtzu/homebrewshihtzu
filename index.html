A little about us

Raised in our home, not a kennel.

We are a very small home breeder of what we believe are one of the best dogs - Shih Tzus. We believe puppies should grow up surrounded by people, attention, and plenty of love.

We only plan a small number of litters each year, allowing us to give each puppy the individual care and attention they deserve.
